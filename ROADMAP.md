# ZAD roadmap

These are proposed priorities, not delivery promises or evidence of existing adoption.

## Initial release — 1.0.0

The manifesto, reusable agreement, prompt library, templates, illustrative examples, local demo, integration notes, and evaluation proposal are included.

## Next: experience before features

Collect independent experience reports from real tasks. Identify unnecessary clarification, missed assumptions, unclear authorisation, and situations where ZAD adds no value. Publish failures as well as successes.

## Then: controlled evaluation

Pilot the four-condition comparison in `evaluation/PROTOCOL.md`. Refine the task set and grading rubric. Publish a versioned protocol before a confirmatory run.

## Tooling only when needed

Consider verified client adapters, optional task-record helpers, and a prompt-review aid if experience demonstrates a useful need. A punctuation-only linter is not a measure of reasoning or safety.

## Community

Welcome translations and relevant prior work. Evolve terminology and governance based on contribution volume and evidence, rather than adding process in advance.
